# Gap Assessment — Product Requirements

## Problem
High-achieving women in executive and entrepreneurial roles often operate in chronic "fight or flight" survival mode — pushing through stress without awareness of their thoughts, feelings, and behaviors. They don't see the gap between where they are and the epic life they desire until they stop to assess it.

## Target User
Female executives and entrepreneurs, ages 35–65, who desire to live an epic life. Busy, results-driven, may not realize how much survival-mode living is costing them.

## Core Objects
- **Life Areas**: Career & Business, Health & Wellness, Relationships & Connection, Finances & Wealth, Personal Growth & Learning, Spirituality & Purpose
- **Assessment**: A session where the user rates each life area on 4 dimensions
- **Assessment Scores**: Per-area ratings — current (1-10), desired (1-10), stress level (1-5), awareness level (1-5)
- **Gap Map**: Computed view showing gap (desired − current), fight/flight flag (stress ≥ 4), low-awareness flag (awareness ≤ 2)

## MVP (v1) — Must-Haves
- [ ] Landing page shows the app with demo Gap Map (no login wall)
- [ ] User starts an assessment covering all 6 life areas
- [ ] For each area: 4 rating questions (current, desired, stress, awareness)
- [ ] Scores persist to the database
- [ ] Gap Map results page: per-area gap scores, fight/flight indicators, awareness levels
- [ ] User can start a new assessment
- [ ] Responsive: sidebar nav on desktop, hamburger on mobile

## Non-Goals (v1)
No user accounts/login. No payment. No coaching content. No community. No AI-generated insights. No progress-over-time tracking.

## Success Criteria
A first-time visitor lands on the homepage, starts an assessment, answers 4 questions across 6 life areas, and sees their personal Gap Map with fight/flight and awareness flags — all without creating an account.
