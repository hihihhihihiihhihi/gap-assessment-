Problem: High-performing women cannot see the distance between the life they are living and the life they want — or that they are running parts of it in fight-or-flight without knowing. The Gap Audit makes that distance visible in three minutes.

For: Female professionals and entrepreneurs, 35–65, who look successful from the outside and are quietly running out of themselves.

Core things to track: For each of six life areas — career, health, relationships, finances, growth, purpose — four readings: where she is now, where she wants to be, how much of it runs on stress, and how aware she is of what she's feeling there. The output is her Gap Map: the areas ranked by priority, with fight/flight and low-awareness zones flagged.

The one workflow (must work v1): A new visitor completes all six areas, sees her Gap Map immediately, and leaves her email to keep it.

Success in a week: Real women are completing the audit and leaving real email addresses — completion rate over drop-off is the number to watch, not signups.

Who will actually use this: Women who read the chapter (or hear the talk), recognised themselves, and want to know how wide their own gap is before they'll talk to anyone about it.

What it replaces or earns: It replaces "I'm fine" with a picture. She gains a named, ranked map of where she is versus where she wants to be; you earn the email of a woman who has just seen her own gap — the warmest possible lead for Phoenix Realm and the Theta Collective.

Build focus: Lean MVP — the six-area audit, the Gap Map result, the email capture. No accounts, no dashboard, no course. Built to test one question: will she finish it and leave her address?
