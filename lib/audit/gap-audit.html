How to use this

Read each statement and choose the number that best reflects how true it has been for you over the past 6 months — not how you'd like it to be, and not how it looks from the outside.

1 — Never true
2 — Rarely true
3 — Sometimes true
4 — Often true
5 — Almost always true

Answer quickly. Your first instinct is usually the honest one.
